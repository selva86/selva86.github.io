# Brief: ST2-13.1 Capstone - A Survey Analysis End to End in R

Industry-grade, end-to-end, publish-worthy for both technical and business
readers (the capstone standard from the TS handbook).

Arc: a realistic employee-engagement or customer survey, ~600 responses,
generated in-chapter with known structure (so every number is reproducible).
1. Design recap: what was asked, scales used, sampling frame, response rate.
2. EDA: response patterns, item distributions, missingness map.
3. Weighting: post-stratification to known population margins; show the
   unweighted-vs-weighted comparison honestly.
4. Likert handling: when means mislead; diverging stacked bars; ordinal tests.
5. Reliability: Cronbach's alpha on the scale items, what it does and misses.
6. Comparisons: groups via appropriate tests, effect sizes, CIs, corrections.
7. The deliverable: an executive-summary section written like a real report
   (three findings, one caveat, one recommendation) plus the technical
   appendix pattern.
Multiple strategies compared where real analysts disagree (weighted vs
unweighted, mean vs ordinal), with a clear recommendation each time.
