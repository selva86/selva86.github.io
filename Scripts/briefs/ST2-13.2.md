# Brief: ST2-13.2 Capstone - A Clinical-Style Group Comparison in R

Industry-grade, end-to-end, publish-worthy for technical and business readers.

Arc: a two-arm comparison in the style of a clinical or program evaluation
(simulated trial data with known effect, so results are checkable).
1. Protocol first: hypothesis, primary and secondary endpoints, alpha,
   planned analysis - written BEFORE seeing data (preregistration in miniature).
2. Power: sample size from a minimally important difference; show the power
   curve and what changing assumptions does to it.
3. Baseline table: the Table-1 pattern with standardized mean differences,
   and why testing baselines is the wrong instinct.
4. Primary analysis: the prespecified test + estimate with CI; intention-to-
   treat framing.
5. Robustness: nonparametric alternative, covariate adjustment via ANCOVA,
   per-protocol sensitivity - three strategies compared against the primary.
6. Honest reporting: effect size with uncertainty in plain language, the
   CONSORT-style flow of participants, what this study cannot claim.
The chapter's spine is the discipline: decided-in-advance vs data-driven,
shown side by side.
