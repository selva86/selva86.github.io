# Brief: ST2-13.3 Capstone - A Business Experiment in R

Industry-grade, end-to-end, publish-worthy for technical and business readers.

Arc: a pricing-page or onboarding-flow experiment at a subscription product,
simulated with realistic conversion rates (1-4%) and traffic, so every
number reproduces.
1. Metric definition: primary metric, guardrails, and why "signups" and
   "revenue per visitor" can disagree.
2. Design: MDE from business stakes, sample size, runtime estimate against
   real traffic; the peeking problem and the committed stopping rule.
3. Monitoring: sample-ratio-mismatch check, guardrail dashboards.
4. Analysis: proportion test + CI for the primary; CUPED-style variance
   reduction as the compared alternative strategy; segment slices with the
   multiple-comparisons discipline applied.
5. The decision memo: one page written for the person who owns the roadmap -
   result, uncertainty, revenue math, recommendation, and what we would run
   next. The memo is the deliverable the chapter builds toward.
Strategies compared honestly (raw test vs CUPED, whole-population vs
segments) with a recommendation each time.
