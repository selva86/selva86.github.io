Product Data Scientist

Design an experiment that survives scrutiny, measure a cause when you cannot randomise, and turn the result into a decision somebody can act on. Finish it and you can tell a product team what to ship, and defend it.

Framing an ambiguous product question · Metric drop diagnosis · Funnel decomposition · Cohort analysis and mix shift · Opportunity sizing
02Metric designChoose a metric that can carry the decision, and a guardrail that protects it.in the plan
Metric taxonomy · The Overall Evaluation Criterion · Guardrail metrics · Goodhart law and metric gaming · Multiple testing across metrics · Proxy metrics and surrogacy · Metric sensitivity · Ratio metrics and the delta method
03Experiment designSize and specify a test that answers the question it was built for.in the plan

04Experiment trustworthinessTell a real result from a broken one before you act on it.in the plan
The A/A test · Sample ratio mismatch · Twyman law · Internal and external validity · Simpson paradox · Peeking and alpha inflation
05Experiment sensitivityGet a trustworthy answer from less traffic, or sooner.in the plan
Variance and statistical sensitivity · Triggering · Variance reduction with CUPED · CUPAC · Sequential testing and always-valid inference · A/B/n tests · Interleaving
06Interference and network effectsRun a valid test when one user affects another.in the plan
SUTVA and no interference · Cluster randomisation · Switchback experiments · Geo experiments · Interference detection
07Heterogeneous treatment effectsFind who an effect is for, and decide who to treat.in the plan
Average and conditional treatment effects · Subgroup analysis and pre-specification · Uplift modelling · Causal forests and honest estimation · Multi-armed bandits · Thompson sampling
08Causal inference without randomisationEstimate an effect you were never able to randomise.in the plan

09Long-term effectsMeasure what a launch does after the first week.in the plan

10Decision-making and communicationTurn a result into a decision, and make it believed.in the plan
