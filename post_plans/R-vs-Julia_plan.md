## Lead Paragraph

Julia runs tight numerical loops faster than R — often 10–100x faster. But most statistical work isn't tight numerical loops, and R ships 21,000+ domain-tested packages, a tooling ecosystem (RStudio, tidyverse, Quarto) nothing in Julia matches, and a community big enough that someone has already answered your question. This guide benchmarks the speed claims honestly, maps the ecosystem gap package-by-package, and gives you a decision rule you can actually use.
