### Core Content Sections (H2 headings)

#### H2 1: "How do you parse dates from text strings?"
- **Theory:** ymd(), mdy(), dmy() family — lubridate's smart parsing
- **Exercise 1:** Parse "2024-03-15" into a Date using ymd()
  - Starter: `my_date <- ___("2024-03-15")`
  - Solution: `my_date <- ymd("2024-03-15")`; output class and value
- **Exercise 2:** Parse "March 15, 2024" and "15/03/2024" into dates
  - Starter: scaffold with mdy() and dmy()
  - Solution: uses mdy() and dmy() correctly
- **Inline Try it:** Parse "07-Jan-2025" into a date (tests dmy with month abbreviation)
- **Callout:** [TIP] lubridate guesses the format from the function name, not the separator

#### H2 2: "How do you extract and modify date components?"
- **Theory:** year(), month(), day(), wday() extractors; replacement forms
- **Exercise 3:** Extract year, month number, and weekday name from `my_date`
  - Starter: scaffold with year(), month(), wday()
  - Solution: year(my_date), month(my_date), wday(my_date, label = TRUE)
- **Exercise 4:** Change the month of `my_date` to December and verify
  - Starter: `month(my_date) <- ___`
  - Solution: `month(my_date) <- 12`; print result
- **Inline Try it:** Extract the quarter from today() (tests quarter())
- **Callout:** [KEY INSIGHT] Component functions are both getters and setters — `month(x)` reads, `month(x) <- 6` writes

#### H2 3: "How do you do arithmetic with dates?"
- **Theory:** days(), months(), years() period functions; addition/subtraction
- **Exercise 5:** Add 90 days and 3 months to "2024-01-31" — compare the results
  - Starter: scaffold with days() and months()
  - Solution: shows that adding 3 months gives April 30, while 90 days gives April 30 too (coincidence) — but try adding 1 month to Jan 31 → Feb 29 (leap year 2024)
- **Exercise 6:** Calculate how many days between "2024-01-01" and "2024-12-31"
  - Starter: `diff_days <- as.numeric(___)`
  - Solution: `as.numeric(ymd("2024-12-31") - ymd("2024-01-01"))` → 365
- **Inline Try it:** What date is 100 days from today()? (tests today() + days())
- **Callout:** [WARNING] Adding months() to the 31st can produce unexpected results — month(result) may jump because Feb/Apr/Jun/Sep/Nov have fewer than 31 days

#### H2 4: "How do you work with date-time objects and timezones?"
- **Theory:** ymd_hms(), with_tz(), force_tz(), Sys.timezone()
- **Exercise 7:** Parse "2024-07-04 14:30:00" as a datetime in UTC and convert to "America/New_York"
  - Starter: scaffold with ymd_hms() and with_tz()
  - Solution: ymd_hms("2024-07-04 14:30:00", tz = "UTC") |> with_tz("America/New_York")
- **Exercise 8:** What is the hour component in both UTC and New York time?
  - Starter: extract hour() from both
  - Solution: UTC = 14, NY = 10 (EDT offset -4)
- **Inline Try it:** Use force_tz() to reinterpret a UTC time as "Asia/Tokyo" — what changes? (tests force_tz vs with_tz)
- **Callout:** [KEY INSIGHT] with_tz() changes display (same instant, different clock), force_tz() changes the instant (same clock, different moment)

#### H2 5: "How do you calculate intervals and durations?"
- **Theory:** interval(), %within%, as.period(), as.duration()
- **Exercise 9:** Create an interval from "2024-01-01" to "2024-06-30" and test whether "2024-03-15" falls within it
  - Starter: scaffold with interval() or %--% and %within%
  - Solution: `int <- ymd("2024-01-01") %--% ymd("2024-06-30"); ymd("2024-03-15") %within% int` → TRUE
- **Exercise 10:** Calculate exact age in years from a birthdate "1995-08-22" to today
  - Starter: scaffold with %--% and %/% years(1)
  - Solution: `(ymd("1995-08-22") %--% today()) %/% years(1)`
- **Inline Try it:** How many complete months are in the interval "2024-03-01" to "2024-11-15"? (tests %/% months(1))
- **Callout:** [TIP] The %--% operator creates an interval; divide by years(1) or months(1) with %/% to get whole units
