## SEO STRATEGY NOTES

1. **Title patterns that rank:** "[Method] in R: [Action Verb] [Specific Outcome]" — e.g., "ANOVA in R: Compare Means Across 3+ Groups Step-by-Step"

2. **Question-form H2s trigger PAA boxes:** Frame every core heading as a question matching "People Also Ask" queries

3. **"vs" comparison posts drive massive traffic:** Every "X vs Y" keyword pair deserves either a dedicated comparison section or its own post

4. **"How to interpret" is the money keyword:** Users who search this are actively analyzing data and will spend 5+ minutes on a page that helps them interpret output

5. **"Assumptions" keywords are gateway traffic:** Users checking assumptions often discover they need a different test entirely → internal links to other sub-paths

6. **Package-name keywords capture expert traffic:** "brms tutorial", "lavaan CFA", "metafor forest plot", "rugarch GARCH" — users searching by package name have high intent

7. **"Step by step" and "with example" modifiers:** Adding these to any method name converts a head keyword into a long-tail with 2-5x higher CTR

8. **Error/troubleshooting keywords are underserved:** "ANOVA assumptions violated what to do", "lmer convergence warning", "heteroscedasticity regression fix" — these have almost zero good results on Google
