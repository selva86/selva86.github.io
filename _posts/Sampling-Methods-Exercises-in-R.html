Exercise 13: Permutation test for two means



Difficulty: Advanced.



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

    
set.seed(1)
obs <- diff(by(mtcars$mpg, mtcars$am, mean))
perms <- replicate(2000, {
  am <- sample(mtcars$am)
  diff(by(mtcars$mpg, am, mean))
})
mean(abs(perms) >= abs(obs))
