Exercise 10: Prune to best cp



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

    
fit <- rpart(mpg ~ ., data = mtcars, cp = 0.001)
best_cp <- fit$cptable[which.min(fit$cptable[,"xerror"]), "CP"]
prune(fit, cp = best_cp)
