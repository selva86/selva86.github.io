Exercise 11: Many models with broom



Difficulty: Advanced.



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

    
iris |>
  group_by(Species) |>
  tidyr::nest() |>
  mutate(model = purrr::map(data, ~ lm(Sepal.Length ~ Petal.Length, .x)),
         tidy = purrr::map(model, tidy)) |>
  tidyr::unnest(tidy)

    

      
      
    

    

  

  








Exercise 12: glance per group



Difficulty: Advanced.



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

    
iris |>
  group_by(Species) |>
  tidyr::nest() |>
  mutate(model = purrr::map(data, ~ lm(Sepal.Length ~ Petal.Length, .x)),
         glance = purrr::map(model, glance)) |>
  tidyr::unnest(glance) |>
  select(Species, r.squared)
