Exercise 4.4: Bar with error bars



Difficulty: Advanced.



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

  group_by(cyl) |>
  summarise(m = mean(mpg), s = sd(mpg)) |>
  ggplot(aes(factor(cyl), m)) +

  geom_errorbar(aes(ymin = m - s, ymax = m + s), width = 0.2)
