Exercise 5: Relative risk



Difficulty: Intermediate.



Show solution



  

    
RInteractive R

    
# 2x2: exposed/disease
m <- matrix(c(40, 60, 20, 80), 2, 2)
risk_exp <- m[1,1] / sum(m[1,])
risk_un  <- m[2,1] / sum(m[2,])
risk_exp / risk_un
