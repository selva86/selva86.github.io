## The repositioning, and why "no AI" makes it stronger

"Free to learn, pay to prove it" is dead. The new wedge, given the decisions above:

**In 2026, AI writes the R. What it cannot give you is the judgment to know the
answer is right, a path that actually gets you there, and proof that you, not a
chatbot, can do it. We are the expert-authored alternative to AI slop.**

This works *because* we are not bolting on an AI tutor. The product is the opposite of
a generic AI: a **curated, sequenced, human-authored path**, **deliberate practice
with real worked solutions**, and a **credential that certifies un-assisted
judgment**. The enemies are clear: scattered free tutorials, watch-and-forget video
courses, and AI-generated slop you cannot trust.

**Lead line to anchor on (replace "pay to prove it"):**

> **"Anyone can prompt an AI to write R. Few can tell when it is wrong. Become one
> of them."**

Supporting framings to test:
- *"The expert-authored path to R you can actually trust."*
- *"Get genuinely good at R, and prove it. No AI shortcuts."*
- *"From following tutorials to fluent, and certified for it."*

The promise is **capability + judgment + proof**, not "a badge."

## How free converts to paid (the engine)

The model is still "sunk effort compounds into a credential you now want," but the
day-to-day pull is **the answer key and the guided structure**, not an AI tutor:

1. **Land -> wow.** The climb + a 60-second runnable win. *I can start now.*
2. **Learn + attempt free.** XP and progress accrue; the climb fills in.
3. **The solution wall (conversion moment 1).** A learner attempts a harder exercise,
   wants the full worked solution and reasoning -> that is Pro. Cheap to provide,
   high intent at the exact moment of need.
4. **The momentum pull (moment 2).** "You are 60% to your certificate; here are your
   three weak spots." Pro turns scattered effort into a finishing plan.
5. **The assessment gate (moment 3).** End of a level: claim the graded result and
   the certificate -> Pro. They have already done the work.
6. **The portfolio pull (moment 4).** The project + the public portfolio page (the CV
   artifact) -> Pro.
7. **Capstone + founding urgency (moment 5).** Six certificates to a capstone, at the
   founding price, before the September launch.

No recurring AI cost anywhere in that funnel. Every paid item is authored once and
sold many times.
