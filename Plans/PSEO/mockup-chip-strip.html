dplyr filter() in R: Subset Rows by Condition

The filter() function in dplyr keeps rows that satisfy a logical condition and drops the rest. You can combine multiple conditions with &, |, !, or by listing them as separate arguments (treated as AND).

By Selva Prabhakaran  ·  Published May 10, 2026  ·  Last updated May 10, 2026
Beginner · 10 min read
QUICK ANSWER (kept as-is): code card, 7 patterns, snippet-eligible.
⚡ Quick Answer
filter(df, mpg > 20)                # single condition
filter(df, mpg > 20, cyl == 4)      # AND
filter(df, mpg > 20 | cyl == 4)     # OR
filter(df, cyl %in% c(4, 6))        # set membership
filter(df, between(hp, 100, 200))   # range
filter(df, !is.na(x), x > 5)        # NA-safe
filter(df, x == max(x), .by = grp)  # by group
