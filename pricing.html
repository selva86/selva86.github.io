The R Foundations and Data Analyst tracks are free in full, every lesson, along with the opening section of all four specializations and the standalone tutorial library of 1,267 tutorials and 27 tools. You can even earn the R Fundamentals certificate at no cost. Pro unlocks the four specializations in full, the graded practice, the projects, your progress dashboard, and the career certificates.

What is the difference between Single Track and All-Access?

Single Track unlocks the shared core plus one specialization of your choice, with grading, and earns you two certificates. All-Access unlocks all four specializations, every certificate, all 27 tools, and the projects, for just $64 more a year. Most people start with All-Access to keep their options open.

Can I upgrade from Single Track to All-Access later?

Anytime, and you only pay the difference for the remainder of your term. Your progress, XP, and any certificate you have already earned carry over untouched.

Can I try before I pay?

Yes. Most of the site is free: every tutorial, the first lessons of every course, and all the tools. When you do upgrade, every paid plan carries a 30-day money-back guarantee: if Pro is not for you, email us within 30 days of your first charge for a full refund, no questions asked.

What happens to my work if I cancel?

Your exercises, certificates, and saved posts stay forever. You simply lose access to Pro-only grading and projects until you resubscribe. Founding members keep their founding price when they return.

Is there a student discount?
