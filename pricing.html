You can even earn the R Fundamentals certificate at no cost. Pro unlocks the four specializations in full, the graded practice, the projects, your progress dashboard, and the career certificates.

What is the difference between Single Track and All-Access?

Single Track unlocks the shared core plus one specialization of your choice, with grading, and earns you two certificates. All-Access unlocks all four specializations, every certificate, all 27 tools, and the projects, for just $34 more a year. Most people start with All-Access to keep their options open.

Can I upgrade from Single Track to All-Access later?

Anytime, and you only pay the difference for the remainder of your term. Your progress, XP, and any certificate you have already earned carry over untouched.
