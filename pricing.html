Why r-statistics Pro exists

The middle layer that did not exist

The serious R training market is split between extremes. Generalist platforms spread thin across ten languages and skim the surface of R. Bootcamps charge $7,000+ for what amounts to a syllabus and a Slack channel. Neither delivers what R practitioners actually need: deep, R-specific instruction with deliberate practice.

r-statistics.co has been the focused R-learning corner of the internet since 2015, with 284 in-depth tutorials and 27 interactive tools serving roughly 478,000 R practitioners annually. Pro extends this foundation with graded exercises, verifiable certifications, real-world projects, and live mentoring.

World-class R depth, runnable in your browser, priced for individual learners. If Pro does not make you a measurably better R user within 30 days, we refund every cent.

The honest answers

Real questions members ask before signing up.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

(Available when Pro launches.) You get every Pro feature, fully unlocked. No card is charged for 7 days, we just hold your payment method. Cancel any time inside the trial and you owe nothing. After day 7 your card is charged the plan you picked, and the 30-day money-back guarantee kicks in from that day.

Can I really cancel any time?

Yes. One click in your billing page. No "are you sure" pop-ups, no retention call. Annual plans don't auto-renew silently, you get a 14-day warning email before each renewal.

How is this different from DataCamp / Coursera?

We do one thing, R, better and cheaper than anyone. The generalists spread their attention across ten languages; we go three layers deeper on R specifically. See the comparison table above.

Will my employer pay for it?

Most do. We'll send you a tailored manager pitch and a tax-deductible invoice. Or move to the Teams plan and let your employer cover the whole team.

I already paid for a year. Can I switch to lifetime?

Yes, we pro-rate. The unused portion of your annual plan is credited against the $199 lifetime price. Email us and we'll do the math.

Do I lose my progress if I downgrade to Free?

Never. Your exercises, certificates, and saved posts stay forever. Pro-only courses become read-only previews.

Is there a student discount?

Yes, 50% off Pro Annual for verified students (.edu email). Verification will be one-click at checkout when Pro launches. Email us in the meantime and we will flag your waitlist record.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Amex, UPI, and PayPal. International customers are processed by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record, handles VAT/GST/sales tax). Indian customers by Razorpay (INR settlement, GST applied).
