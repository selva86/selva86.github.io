The grammar of graphics

Every ggplot2 chart is built from the same six layers: data, aesthetic mappings, geoms, statistical transforms, scales, and coordinate systems. Once you internalise this grammar, every chart type collapses into the same mental model — just swap the geom.

This is what makes ggplot2 both harder to learn than base R plotting and far more powerful once you do. The tutorial covers each layer with runnable examples and exercises, then sends you onward to deeper recipes per chart type below.

Aesthetic mappings & geoms

The aesthetic mapping links columns of your data to visual properties — x position, colour, size, shape. The geom decides what visual marks to draw. Combine these two and you have ggplot2 in one sentence.
