Roadmap structure · 4 options

A shared foundation, then equal specializations

Same content in all four. The difference is how the structure is drawn so that Machine Learning, Time Series, Research and Advanced R read as parallel, equal destinations, not as one role stacked above another. Flip between them.

A. The branch map

transit-line feel
Core: foundations ML / Data Scientist Time Series Research / Stats Advanced R / Engineer

Why it works

Reads like a transit map: one shared line, then four equal branches fanning out. Directional and journey-like, nothing is above anything, and everything stays in one view.

Watch out

Branch lines can get busy with real lesson detail. Best as the top-level overview that links into each track.

B. The summit range

keeps the climb

Why it works

Keeps the emotional "climb" you liked, but resolves it into a range of equal peaks instead of one summit. A shared base camp, then a peak for every goal, none taller.

Watch out

Pretty but lower information density; works as a hero image above a detailed list, not as the detailed structure itself.

C. Core and orbits

radial, no top

Why it works

The most explicitly non-hierarchical: a central core with four specializations orbiting at equal distance. Impossible to read as "above." The outer ring frames the capstone naturally.

Watch out

Radial layouts are harder to make responsive and harder to expand into a long, scannable lesson list. Strong as a concept image, weaker as the working navigation.
