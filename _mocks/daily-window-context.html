With nothing closed, the last two rows vanish and the table is four neutral facts.

Conversion leverLoss quantified and extrapolated. The strongest argument of the five because it is arithmetic, not persuasion.
ToneCandid, almost blunt. Closest to the owner's own voice.
Trade-offThe extrapolation could read as a scare if the rate is high. It must be computed from their real history, never a default, or it becomes exactly the manipulation it is trying to avoid.

Option E

Reopen one

Give something back first. One closed lesson reopened each month, free, no conditions.

Reopened one already this month. Pro opens all four now, and everything after.
Conversion leverReciprocity. The free reopen costs nothing and earns goodwill; the person who wants a second one has told you they want the product.
ToneGenerous. The only option that gives before it asks.
Trade-offThe only option needing new state: one reopen per user per month must be recorded and enforced. Also the only one that could be gamed, though at one a month the exposure is trivial.

All five render from data that already exists. /api/me/shelf already returns the open lessons with a closes_at timestamp; it discards the sends that fall outside the window, which is the only thing that needs adding. Option E alone needs new state.

Options are not exclusive. C is a rail and D is a panel, so the natural combination is C in the player with D or A behind the "see all" link.
