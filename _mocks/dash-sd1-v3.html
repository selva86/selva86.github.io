Design notes v3: the brand system applied
  • Paper and navy: page on the gold-standard mock's #fbfaf7 paper; display type in brand navy #1c2c4f; #2056d2 appears ONLY on clickable things. Warm hairlines (#e4e2da) replace app-gray.
  • The accent ritual: the hello uses the site's track-hero signature: Inter Tight 700 with a 900-italic em in the active track's hue.
  • summary(you): a real R console line in IBM Plex Mono on the site's code background, echoing your actual numbers as #> output. Mono is legal here because it IS R code.
  • Data-ink everywhere: a 90-day XP sparkline under the greeting; the week as an actual bar chart with a legend; every stat tile carries its own trend line; nothing decorative is dataless.
  • The mark grammar: track rows wear the same stroke-drawn marks as the Courses dropdown (prompt, bars, bell, forecast, tree, chevrons, gauge). All emoji replaced with stroke icons from the site's symbol style.
  • Certificates as artifacts: gold-radial seals with an inner ring, warm-tinted card, public IDs in tabular numerals.
  • Small-caps kickers (.15em) carry every card label, the mock-v3 voice; section rules with a track-colored dot.
  • The site's close: the page ends on the dark #101a30 footer band with the R. wordmark, like every other page on the site.
  • Motion discipline: one staggered 300ms rise per card, bars fill once, reduced-motion respected, nothing loops.
  • Typography: body IBM Plex Sans (the site's body face; v2 wrongly used Inter), titles Inter Tight, mono only inside the console.
  • Content and organization unchanged from v2; dark mode carried over with mock-v3's night palette.
