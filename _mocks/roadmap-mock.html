The questions everyone asks

Q.Which certificate should I attempt first?

Whichever path is Stage 1 of your roadmap — that's R Foundations for everyone. You don't choose a certificate; you finish a path and the certificate follows. Do them in stage order.

Q.What order should I do the exercises in?

You don't sequence them yourself. Each path interleaves its exercise hubs between lesson clusters, so you practise a topic right after learning it. Just follow the path top to bottom.

Q.Can I skip Stage 1 if I already know R?

Yes. Take the 5-minute placement check — if you pass, the roadmap marks Stage 1 done and starts you at Stage 2. When in doubt, skim it; foundations are quick to confirm and expensive to miss.

Q.Do I have to go in order?

The order is a recommendation, not a lock — every page is open. But each stage assumes the one before it, so following the sequence is the fastest route. The roadmap always shows your single best next step.

Q.Where do the tools fit in?

The 30 interactive tools are companions, not a stage. Reach for a calculator or explorer whenever a lesson or exercise calls for one — they speed up the work but aren't part of the certification track.
