A note from the desk, on building over watching

You do not learn R by watching someone else use it. You learn it by getting stuck on your own data and working out. So I organized everything around things you actually ship: a cleaned dataset, an EDA report, a model, a forecast, a defended analysis, a packaged tool.

The tutorials are still all here, free, in the right order. But now they have a destination. Every one of them exists because the next build needs it.
