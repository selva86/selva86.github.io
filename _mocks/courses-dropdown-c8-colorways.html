C8 colorways

The C8 layout is locked: three stages, monogram, name, promise, certificate line, Start free. These four panels change only the box treatment, each chasing vibrancy through a different mechanism: full chroma, directional color, dark contrast, and saturated pastel with a structural accent.

W1 · Full-color mosaic

Maximum vibrancy: every box IS its track color. White type, reversed white monogram, translucent-white certificate line. The panel becomes a confident color mosaic in the site's seven track hues, the way Spotify and Duolingo treat category tiles. Nothing on the site will look bolder.

Trade-off: the heaviest of the four; it dominates the navbar moment and leaves no quiet place for the eye. Hover darkens rather than brightens.

W2 · Gradient wash

Vibrancy through direction, not weight. Each box carries a diagonal wash of its own hue, rich at the top-left corner and fading toward the CTA. The color moves, so the panel feels alive at rest, and the field stays light enough for ink titles and full-hue accents. Closest in spirit to the C6 you liked, with energy added.

Trade-off: gradients are the current SaaS default (Linear, Stripe); it is the least distinctive of the four.

W3 · Midnight

Vibrancy through contrast: dark tinted boxes, luminous accents. Each box is its hue mixed into near-black, so all seven read as siblings of one premium family; the certificate name and Start free glow in a brightened version of the hue. Echoes the site's dark footer, and no education site's dropdown looks like this.

Trade-off: the boldest identity statement; dark boxes inside a light navbar is a deliberate look you either commit to or don't. Small text on dark needs the contrast checked once on real screens.

W4 · Candy pastel + base bar

The current direction, pushed until it commits. The field jumps to a 30% pastel that is unmistakably colored rather than washed, and a solid 3px bar of the full hue grounds each box at its base. Certificate names and Start free take the deep tone for legibility on the stronger field. Vibrant, but the type stays ink-on-light.

Trade-off: the base bar reintroduces one structural element (a deliberate accent, not the hairline border you removed); at 30% the field is close to the point where ink titles start fighting the color.
