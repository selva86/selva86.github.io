The honest answers

What's the difference between Single Certificate and All-Access?

Single Certificate is a one-time payment for one track, and that credential is yours forever, no subscription. All-Access is a yearly program covering every track, unlimited graded practice, and every track that ships later. If you want more than one credential, All-Access is far cheaper.

Can I buy a Single Certificate and upgrade later?

Yes. If you upgrade to All-Access within your first year, we credit what you paid for the single track toward it.

What does "Pay once" mean on All-Access?

A lifetime option: pay one time and keep All-Access, including every future track, for as long as the site is online. It is capped at the first 200 founding members.

Is the certificate permanent if I cancel?

Yes. Any certificate you have earned stays yours and verifiable forever, whether or not you keep renewing.

What can I actually earn at launch?

The R Fundamentals certificate first, earned by solving real, graded R. Tidyverse, Visualization, Statistics, and Machine Learning follow, included in All-Access as they ship.

Student discount and payment methods?

50% off any plan for verified students (.edu email), one-click at checkout. Cards, UPI, and PayPal, in your local currency, with VAT and GST handled automatically.
