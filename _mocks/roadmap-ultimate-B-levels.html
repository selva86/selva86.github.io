A note from the desk, on levels and rank

I called these levels because each one is genuinely harder than the last, and finishing one earns the next. But a level is a stage of mastery, not a tier of people. A Beginner is not below a Specialist; they are earlier on the same road.

That is why specialization lives inside Levels 3 and 4, and why every track that branches there is equal. The level tells you how far you have come. The track tells you which way you went.
