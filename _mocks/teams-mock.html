Team FAQ

Can I add or remove seats mid-year?

Yes. Add at the pro-rated price any time. If someone leaves, free up their seat in the admin dashboard, we credit the unused portion against your next invoice.

What's the smallest team you'll sell to?

Five seats. Below that, individual Pro is the better fit (and we'll cheerfully tell you so).

Can I run a pilot before committing?

Yes, 14 days, up to 10 seats, no card, full admin dashboard. Most teams pilot two weeks and roll out org-wide in the third.

How does invoicing work?

Annual invoicing in USD, EUR, GBP, INR, or AUD. Net-30 by default; we can do Net-60 on Enterprise. Payment by card, ACH, SEPA, or wire.

Are you SOC 2 compliant?

Yes, SOC 2 Type II. We share the report under NDA on Growth and above. GDPR-ready; DPA on request.

Can content live behind our SSO only?

Yes. SSO-only access, IP allowlisting, and SCIM provisioning are standard on Growth and Enterprise.
