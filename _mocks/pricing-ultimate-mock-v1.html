Questions, answered

Can I really learn everything for free?

Yes. All 284 tutorials and 27 tools are free to read and run, forever. Pro adds graded practice, projects, certificates, and your progress dashboard. The teaching is never paywalled, only the proof.

What is the difference between Single Track and All-Access?

Single Track unlocks the shared core plus one role track of your choice, and earns you two certificates. All-Access unlocks all three tracks, every certificate, all 27 tools, and the projects, for just $13 more a year. Most people start with All-Access to keep their options open.

Can I upgrade from Single Track to All-Access later?

Anytime, and you only pay the difference for the remainder of your term. Your progress, XP, and any certificate you have already earned carry over untouched.

How does the free trial work?

All-Access Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. You get every feature fully unlocked. No charge for 7 days; cancel any time inside the trial and you owe nothing.

What happens to my work if I cancel?

Your exercises, certificates, and saved posts stay forever. You simply lose access to Pro-only grading and projects until you resubscribe. Founding members keep their founding price when they return.

Is there a student discount?

Yes, 50% off any annual plan with a verified .edu email. Verification is one click at checkout.
