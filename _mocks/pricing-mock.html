Why r-statistics Pro exists

Hi, I'm Selva Prabhakaran. I've been writing R tutorials on this site since 2015 — back when "Linear Regression in R" was my first ever post. Today the site hosts 284 in-depth tutorials and reaches roughly 478,000 readers a year.

The reason Pro exists is simple. The serious R resources out there are either too shallow (five-minute YouTube clips that leave you Googling for hours) or absurdly expensive (a $7,000 bootcamp for what amounts to a glorified syllabus and a Slack channel).

r-statistics Pro is the middle path I always wanted I could point readers to: world-class depth, runnable everywhere, for the price of one R book a year. If it doesn't deliver, I'll refund you personally — no forms, no questions, just a reply to your email.
