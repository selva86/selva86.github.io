Track marks, framed

Fixes from your last look: the Data Scientist tree is redrawn with elbow connectors and a filled root so it reads as a decision tree; the ML Engineer pipeline is now a rail with stations sitting on it (terminal station filled = shipped), so the links cannot float; and every mark now lives in a chip. Two chip finishes below — the artwork is identical in both.

F1 · White icon tile

Each mark sits on a small white tile with a soft shadow — the dashboard convention (Stripe, Linear stat cards). On the tinted field the white tile pops cleanly, the artwork gains a stage, and the card gets a crisp secondary anchor opposite the monogram.

Trade-off: the white tile is a neutral frame; the hue appears only in the artwork itself.

F2 · Tinted tile

The same chip in a deeper wash of the track hue — tone-on-tone, so the artwork sits inside its own color family instead of on white. Softer than F1, more color per card, no shadows.
