Questions

Frequently asked

Does the certificate cost anything?

No. Earning a certificate is free. You solve the track's exercises, pass the timed assessment, and the credential is minted automatically, no proctoring fee, no exam voucher.

How is the assessment graded?

The assessment is code-based, not multiple-choice. You write real R that runs against hidden test cases and is auto-graded. You need to demonstrate working command of the track's skills, not memorise trivia.

What does "verifiable" actually mean?

Each certificate gets a unique ID (e.g. RST-2026-A7K3F9) and a public page anyone can load to confirm the holder, track, score and issue date. The credential is also emitted as Open Badges 3.0 JSON signed by the r-statistics.co issuer, so it imports into badge wallets and LinkedIn.

Do I have to solve every exercise in a track?

You need to solve at least 80% of a track's total exercises across its hubs to unlock the assessment. Exercises you solved earlier, even before you decided to pursue the track, count toward that threshold.

Can certificates be revoked?

Yes. In rare cases of integrity violations a credential can be revoked, at which point its public verification page reflects the revoked status. This is what keeps the credential trustworthy for employers.
