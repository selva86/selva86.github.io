Still wondering

The questions everyone asks

Q.How is this different from just browsing the 1,333 tutorials?

The tutorials are the library; the roadmap is the route through it. Instead of 1,333 pages in no particular order, you get a handful of stages in the right order for your goal, each with the exact lessons, the exercise hub that checks them, and the certificate at the end.

Q.What order should I do the exercises in?

You don't sequence them yourself. Each stage interleaves its exercise hubs between lesson clusters, so you practise a topic right after learning it. Just follow the stage top to bottom.

Q.Can I skip Stage 1 if I already know R?

Yes. Take the 5-minute placement check, if you pass, the roadmap marks Stage 1 done and starts you at Stage 2. When in doubt, skim it; foundations are quick to confirm and expensive to miss.

Q.Do I have to go in order?

The order is a recommendation, not a lock, every page is open. But each stage assumes the one before it, so following the sequence is the fastest route. The roadmap always surfaces your single best next step.

Q.Where do the 27 tools fit in?

The interactive tools are companions, not a stage. Each stage points you to the tool that speeds up its work, a distribution explorer, a regression calculator, a sample-size planner, but they aren't part of the certification track.
