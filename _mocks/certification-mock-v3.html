Why this credential is built the way it is

Most certificates are theatre. You pay, you click through a multiple-choice quiz, and you walk away with a PDF that proves you can recognise the right answer when it is one of four. Hiring managers know it, which is exactly why so many of them ignore the line on a resume.

At r-statistics.co we built the opposite. There is no question you can guess. You write real R, it runs against tests you cannot see, and it either passes or it does not. The credential is the receipt for work already done. If you can hold it up, it means you can do the thing, and the public verification page lets anyone confirm that in one click.

01

How the credential is earned

free · no proctor fee · no guessing

One path, three gates, and the numbers are the real ones. The example below is the Tidyverse Practitioner track: 12 exercise hubs, 367 graded exercises, an 80% bar before the assessment unlocks.

1

Work the hubs to 80%

Each track is a cluster of the exercise hubs already on this site. Solve at least 80% of a track's exercises across its hubs at your own pace. Anything you cracked earlier, even before you decided to chase the track, counts toward the bar.

2

Sit the code-based assessment

Reaching the bar unlocks a timed, proctored-style assessment. You write real R that is auto-graded against hidden test cases, not a bank of multiple-choice questions. There is no answer key to recognise, which is the entire point.

Mint the verifiable credential

Pass, and your credential mints with a unique ID like RST-2026-A7K3F9, a public verification page that never expires, and an Open Badges 3.0 record signed by the r-statistics.co issuer that imports straight into LinkedIn.

05

Straight answers

Does it cost anything?

No. Earning a credential is free. You solve the exercises, pass the assessment, and it mints automatically. No proctoring fee, no exam voucher, no upsell at the finish line.

So what is Pro for, then?

The tutorials and the credential are free, and they always will be. Pro is for the things around the work: progress that follows you across devices, your full attempt history, and the dashboard that tracks every track at once. You never have to pay to be certified. You pay, if you want, to make the journey more comfortable.

How is the assessment graded?

It is code, not multiple choice. You write real R that runs against hidden test cases and is auto-graded. There is no answer key to recognise, which is exactly the point.

Do I have to solve every exercise in a track?

You need at least 80% of a track's exercises solved across its hubs before the assessment unlocks. Anything you cracked earlier, even before you decided to chase the track, counts toward that 80%.

What does "verifiable" actually mean?

Each credential gets a unique ID like RST-2026-A7K3F9 and a public page anyone can load to confirm the holder, track, score and issue date. It is also emitted as Open Badges 3.0 JSON signed by the r-statistics.co issuer, so it imports into badge wallets and LinkedIn.

Can a credential be revoked?

Yes. In rare cases of an integrity violation, a credential can be revoked, and its public page then shows that status. That possibility is part of why the credential is worth holding in the first place.
