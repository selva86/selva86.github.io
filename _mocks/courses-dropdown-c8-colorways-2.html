C8 colorways, round 2

Round 1 chased pigment: how much color to pour into the field. That axis tops out at "tinted." This round changes the mechanism instead — light, brand geometry, organic two-hue color, and chromatic typography. Layout stays locked C8: sharp corners, no borders, monogram, certificate line, Start free.

D1 · Lit from within

Vibrancy as light, not paint. A saturated aura radiates from the monogram corner and dissolves into a near-white field, so each card reads as glowing rather than tinted; the monogram casts a soft colored shadow like a lit lamp. On hover the light swells. This is how Raycast and Apple Music make color feel alive without ever flooding a surface.

Trade-off: the effect lives in the top-left corner; the lower half of each card stays quiet by design.

D2 · The dot

Your own logomark, made the vibrancy engine. The site's mark is "R." — so every card carries a large full-saturation dot in its track's hue, cropped by the top-right corner, with a subtle sheen for dimension. Full-strength color in a bold graphic area, text on calm light ground, and the whole system is unmistakably yours: nobody else's dropdown can do this, because nobody else's logo is a dot.

Trade-off: the dot occupies the top-right of every card, so long track names must stay short (they all do today).

D3 · Aurora

Two hues per card, blooming from opposite corners. Each track's color meets a hand-picked companion hue (blue meets sky, pink meets violet, amber meets rose) in soft radial blooms that blend across the card like Stripe's mesh backgrounds. The monogram takes the same duotone as a gradient chip. Organic, expensive-looking color that a flat tint can never produce.

Trade-off: introduces seven companion hues to govern; the palette doubles, and cheap execution of this look reads decorative, so the blend percentages have to stay exactly this restrained.

D4 · Chroma type

The color moves into the typography. Track names set larger and heavier in the full hue, each finished with a solid color dash — the vibrancy is carried by saturated type on a whisper-tinted field, the way editorial design (Pentagram, Bloomberg) makes color feel intentional rather than decorative. The quietest field of the four, and the most confident.

Trade-off: vibrancy concentrates in the name; if your eye wants colored surface, this will read restrained next to D1-D3.
