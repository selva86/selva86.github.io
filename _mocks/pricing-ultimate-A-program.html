The honest answers

Real questions, answered plainly, before you join the waitlist.

What can I actually earn at launch?

The R Fundamentals certificate, first. You earn it by solving the track's exercises (real, graded R, not multiple choice) and it becomes a public, verifiable credential with your name on it. Tidyverse, Visualization, Statistics, and Machine Learning tracks follow, and the Program includes them as they ship.

Is the certificate permanent if I cancel?

Yes. Any certificate you have earned stays yours and stays verifiable forever, whether or not you keep renewing. You are paying to earn credentials and to keep practicing, not renting the ones you already hold.

Why a yearly program and not a one-time fee?

So one price covers every track as it launches plus unlimited graded practice, rather than nickel-and-diming you per certificate. A one-time, per-certificate option is coming after launch for people who only want a single track.

What happens during the 7-day free trial?

(Available from launch, September 1, 2026.) Every feature unlocked, no charge for 7 days, we just hold your payment method. Cancel any time in the trial and you owe nothing. After day 7 your card is charged and the 30-day money-back guarantee begins.

How is this different from DataCamp or Coursera?

Two ways. We go deep on R specifically instead of spreading across ten languages, and our credential is evidence-based: you earn it by solving real R, and anyone can verify it. Theirs are completion badges for finishing the material.

Will my employer pay for it?

Often, yes. We will send a short manager pitch and a proper invoice you can expense. A dedicated Teams plan (admin dashboard, bulk credentials, reporting) is coming after launch.

Is there a student discount, and which payment methods work?

Yes, 50% off for verified students (.edu email), one-click at checkout when we open. Cards, UPI, and PayPal, charged in your local currency, with VAT and GST handled automatically, no surprise tax bills.
