2How R coerces types

R won't let different types share a vector. When you mix them, it silently coerces everything up a ladder: logical, integer, double, complex, character. Character always wins.

Key insight

Coercion is silent, which is what makes it dangerous. When a result looks wrong, class() your inputs first.
