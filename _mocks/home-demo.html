Questions people ask before they start

Is it free?

All 1,300 tutorials, the tools, and the Data Analyst and R Foundations tracks are free, with graded exercises and a certificate. The other four tracks are Pro.

Do I need to install R?

No. Every lesson runs R in your browser, on the page. Install RStudio later, when you want to work on your own files.

I have never programmed. Where do I start?

The demo above starts from the first line. After it, the New-to-R foundations take you to your first real analysis in a week of short sessions.

Are the certificates worth anything?

Each one is earned by passing graded exercises and a project, not by watching videos, and has a public verification page you can send to an employer.

How long does a track take?

The Data Analyst track is about 40 hours of lessons and practice. At three hours a week that is roughly three months.
