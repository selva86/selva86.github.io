Design notes: what moved, what's new, what's gone
  • Kept, everything live today: streak chip in the nav, contextual hello, Today's set with bonus and difficulty chips, reading resume with scroll percent, open windowed lessons with 72h countdowns and series position, the mini-course catalog with the Pro wall (owner rule: every course visible), all stat tiles, tracks with earned and ready-to-claim states, certificates with share links, saved posts, the Pro upsell (Team card takes this slot for seat holders), opt-in card logic, the ?start course-first mode.
  • Unified: the three competing continue surfaces (reading resume, course resume, open lesson) become ONE "Continue" rail with the active track primary. One decision, not three.
  • Fixed: Continue learning previously hardcoded only New to R and Data Analyst; the rail and tracks now cover all tracks including Data Scientist's 178 lessons, and ML Engineer joins the tracks list.
  • New: streak freezes surfaced (they exist in the backend, shown nowhere); weekly summary numbers in the hero; the recap card; the practice-meter tile (renders only when flag:exercise-meter is on); review-type task marked in Today's set.
  • Gone: the "Recommended next" card. Its three static rows are replaced by the set's reasons and the continue rail; the Pro row lives in the upsell already. Nothing informational was lost.
  • Contained: the catalog becomes a horizontal shelf instead of a page-dominating grid, so a free user's own progress stays above the sell.
