Design notes: what moved, what's new, what's gone
  • Organizing idea: the dashboard IS the path. The active track becomes a full spine with section rungs, the current position highlighted, and the certificate always visible at the summit with an honest time estimate. Certificates justify Pro better than any banner.
  • Kept: every live element: streak chip, hello, Today's set with bonus, reading resume and open windowed lessons (merged into one "Also in flight" list with countdowns), full tracks list with earned states, catalog with the Pro wall, stats, certificates with share, saved, upsell/Team slot, opt-in logic, ?start mode (it would open with the spine on New to R lesson 1).
  • New: freezes note under the set; paused-track honesty ("paused 12 days, resume any time"); time-to-certificate estimate; meter tile flag-gated.
  • Gone: "Recommended next" (the spine IS the recommendation) and the big stat band (numbers compress into the rail; the page leads with direction, not totals).
  • Risk to note: multi-track users see one spine; the chooser is the tracks card below. If analytics show heavy parallel-tracking, SD1 or SD4 serve that shape better.
