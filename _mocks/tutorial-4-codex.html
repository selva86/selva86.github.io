R Data Types: Which Type Is Your Variable?

R has six basic data types, numeric, integer, character, logical, complex, and raw, and the type of a value decides what you can do with it.

Beginner30 min10 exercises SPSelva PrabhakaranSince 2016

What are R's six data types?

Every value in R belongs to exactly one of six basic types, and one call to class() tells you which.

Six values, six types. weight is numeric (decimal), age is integer only because of the L, name is character (quoted). The literal decides the type.

How does R coerce types?

R won't let different types share a vector. Mix them and it silently coerces up a ladder: logical, integer, double, complex, character. Character always wins.

Warning

Coercion is silent. R won't warn you that your numbers became strings, you find out when arithmetic breaks. When a result looks wrong, class() your inputs first.
