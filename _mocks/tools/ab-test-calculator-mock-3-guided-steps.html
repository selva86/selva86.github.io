iNew to A/B testing? Read the 4-minute primer

What it is. Route traffic to two variants, measure a binary outcome, ask whether B’s true rate really differs from A’s or it’s sampling jitter.

How to read it. Lift = B−A. p-value = chance of a gap this big under no difference. A 95% CI crossing zero can’t rule out “no effect”. BF10 > 10 favours B.

Frequentist vs Bayesian. Frequentist sets alpha + power and decides on p. Bayesian updates a prior to a Beta posterior and reports P(B>A) + a credible interval. Same question, different assumptions.

Anatomy of an A/B test the math, 5 steps
p_pool=(c_a+c_b)/(n_a+n_b) SE_0=sqrt(p_pool(1-p_pool)(1/n_a+1/n_b)) z=(p_b-p_a)/SE_0 ; p=2(1-Φ(|z|))
Two-proportion z-test. Pool under H0, compute SE, read z. CI uses unpooled SE. Matches prop.test(...,correct=FALSE).
prior Beta(α₀,β₀) → posterior Beta(α₀+c,β₀+n-c) output P(p_b>p_a), 95% CrI
Beta-Binomial conjugate. Posterior is Beta in closed form. Sample 10,000 draws, count P(B>A), take 2.5/97.5 percentiles of the lift.
interim look at fraction t; spend α_t, sum=α (Pocock)
Sequential alpha-spending. Peeking K times inflates false positives; Pocock / O’Brien-Fleming spread alpha across looks.
h=2(arcsin√p2-arcsin√p1); n=(z_α+z_β)²/h²
Sample size for proportions. Arcsine transform (Cohen’s h). n per arm at 50/50; scale by 1/(4k(1-k)) for unequal. Matches pwr.2p.test().
absolute=p_b-p_a; relative=(p_b-p_a)/p_a; RR=p_b/p_a
Lift vs RR vs absolute. 10%→12% is +2pp absolute, +20% relative, RR=1.20. CIs/BF10 on the absolute scale.
When this is the wrong tool 6 alternatives
If you have…
Use instead
Three or more variants
Multi-armed bandit, or Bonferroni / Holm over pairwise z-tests.
Continuous outcome
Welch’s two-sample t-test. The proportion test forces a binary outcome.
Survival / time-to-event
Kaplan-Meier + log-rank, or Cox PH.
Repeated measures on same user
Paired test (McNemar / paired t) or mixed-effects.
Ratio metrics
Delta-method or bootstrap CI for the ratio.
You peeked early & stopped
Group-sequential design, or always-valid intervals (mSPRT).
