iNew to A/B testing? Read the 4-minute primer

What it is. An A/B test routes traffic to two variants, measures a binary outcome, and asks whether B’s true rate differs from A’s or the gap is random sampling jitter.

How to read it. Lift = B−A. p-value = chance of a gap this big under no difference. 95% CI crossing zero means you can’t rule out “no effect”. BF10 > 10 favours B, < 1/10 favours A.

Frequentist vs Bayesian. Frequentist: pre-set alpha + power, decide on p < alpha. Bayesian: prior → Beta posterior → P(B>A) and a credible interval. Same question, different assumptions.

Plain-English inference what it means

We compared your two groups using a two-proportion z-test (frequentist) and a beta-binomial Bayesian model side by side. B’s rate of 14.40% is 2.40pp higher than A’s 12.00%. Frequentist: not significant (p = 0.1129, 95% CI [−0.57, 5.37]pp). Bayesian: a 94.5% probability B beats A.

Anatomy of an A/B test the math, 5 steps
p_pool = (c_a+c_b)/(n_a+n_b) SE_0 = sqrt(p_pool(1-p_pool)(1/n_a+1/n_b)) z = (p_b-p_a)/SE_0 ; p = 2(1-Φ(|z|))
Two-proportion z-test. Pool under H0, compute SE, read off z. CI uses the unpooled SE. Matches prop.test(..., correct=FALSE).
prior p_a,p_b ~ Beta(α₀,β₀) posterior p ~ Beta(α₀+c, β₀+n-c) output P(p_b>p_a), 95% CrI
Beta-Binomial conjugate. Beta prior is conjugate to Binomial; posterior is Beta in closed form. Sample 10,000 draws, count P(B>A), take 2.5/97.5 percentiles of the lift.
each interim look at fraction t of N spend α_t such that sum α_t = α (Pocock)
Sequential alpha-spending. Peeking K times and stopping early inflates false positives. Pocock / O’Brien-Fleming spread alpha across looks to hold family-wise error.
h = 2(arcsin√p2 - arcsin√p1) n_per_arm = (z_α+z_β)²/h²
Sample size for proportions. Arcsine transform (Cohen’s h). Returns n per arm at 50/50; scale by 1/(4k(1-k)) for unequal splits. Matches pwr.2p.test().
absolute lift = p_b-p_a (pp) relative lift = (p_b-p_a)/p_a risk ratio = p_b/p_a
Lift vs RR vs absolute. 10%→12% is +2pp absolute, +20% relative, RR=1.20. CIs and BF10 are on the absolute scale.
When this is the wrong tool 6 alternatives
If you have…
Use instead
Three or more variants
Multi-armed bandit (Thompson sampling), or Bonferroni / Holm over pairwise z-tests.
Continuous outcome (revenue, time)
Welch’s two-sample t-test. The two-proportion test forces a binary outcome.
Survival / time-to-event
Kaplan-Meier + log-rank, or Cox PH. Censoring breaks the binomial.
Repeated measures on same user
Paired test (McNemar / paired t), or mixed-effects with user random intercept.
Ratio metrics (clicks-per-visit)
Delta-method or bootstrap CI. The denominator is random per user.
You peeked early & stopped
Group-sequential (alpha-spending), or always-valid intervals (mSPRT).
