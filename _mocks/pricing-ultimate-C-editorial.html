The honest answers
What can I actually earn at launch?

The R Fundamentals certificate first, earned by solving the track's exercises (real, graded R, not multiple choice). It becomes a public, verifiable credential with your name on it. Tidyverse, Visualization, Statistics, and Machine Learning follow, included in the Program as they ship.

Is the certificate permanent if I cancel?

Yes. Any certificate you have earned stays yours and verifiable forever, whether or not you keep renewing. You are paying to earn credentials and to keep practicing, not renting the ones you already hold.

Why a yearly program and not a one-time fee?

So one price covers every track as it launches plus unlimited graded practice. A one-time, per-certificate option follows after launch for people who only want a single track.

How is this different from DataCamp or Coursera?

Two ways: we go deep on R specifically instead of spreading across ten languages, and our credential is evidence-based, earned by solving real R and verifiable by anyone. Theirs are completion badges.

Student discount and payment methods?

50% off for verified students (.edu email), one-click at checkout. Cards, UPI, and PayPal, in your local currency, with VAT and GST handled automatically.
