        Anatomy of a confidence interval
      
      
    
        
    
          
    CI = estimate ± critical · SE
    
          
    The general form. Most CIs decompose into a point estimate, a critical value from a sampling distribution (z, t, χ²), and a standard error.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    SE_mean = s / √n
df = n − 1
critical = t1−α/2, df
    
          
    Mean. Use the t-distribution. For n > 30 the t and z criticals are essentially identical.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Wilson score CI for p:
center = p̂ + z²/(2n)
half = z·√(p̂(1−p̂)/n + z²/(4n²))
CI = (center ± half) / (1 + z²/n)
    
          
    Proportion. Wilson is the right default — stays inside [0, 1], performs well near boundaries.
