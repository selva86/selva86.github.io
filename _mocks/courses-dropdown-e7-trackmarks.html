E7 · Per-track marks

E3's idea, matured into a system: every track wears the chart it teaches. Seven miniature data-graphics in one shared stroke grammar — same weight, same corner, same size — so they read as a family, while each one means something to the person choosing: the analyst sees the bar chart they will build, the researcher sees the bell curve, the forecaster sees the line that turns dashed where the future starts.

Trade-off: seven bespoke glyphs to maintain instead of one; a new track someday needs a new mark drawn in the same grammar.
