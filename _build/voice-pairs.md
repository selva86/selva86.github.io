# Owner voice: before -> after pairs (verbatim from Plans/01_email_and_nurture/owner-voice-pack.md)

These are the owner's own edits of machine prose. The voice pass imitates the
direction of each edit; it does not follow rules. Keep this file in step with
the voice pack when the owner adds edits.

- "Say you test" -> "Let's say you test"
- "Picture a coffee shop" -> "Let's consider a coffee shop's daily sales and you observe this:"
- "Nine right. One wrong." -> "She gets nine right and one wrong."
- "It does not, and most people..." -> "It does not." (own line) then "And most people..." (next line)
- "That is the whole secret" -> "That is the core idea."
- "alphabet soup" -> "soup"
- "the right test falls out the bottom" -> "you will have the right test standing in front of you"
- "patterns jump out on their own" -> "patterns make sense and the exact mechanism behind it is crystal clear"
- "This one tends to live in an open tab for years." -> (deleted)
- "is the difference real?" -> "is the difference real?. In other words, does one store actually do better than the other?"
- "feel automatic" -> "feel at home"
- "play with" -> "work with"
- "The question is the ordinary one anybody asks of three numbers like that:" -> "The question is an ordinary one anyone would want to be answered:"
- "count how often it fakes a result that good" -> "know how often you get a result that good"
- "run the same three moves somewhere else" -> "apply the method somewhere else entirely"
- "Priya tastes all ten and calls them one at a time." -> "Next, Priya tastes all ten and calls them one at a time."
- "So is Priya skilled, or just lucky?" -> "Now, is Priya really skilled, or just got lucky?"
- "Nine out of ten does feel like a lot. The trouble is that..." -> "Getting nine out of ten right does feel like a lot. However, the trouble is that..."
- "And you can just go and find out. Press the buttons below." -> "How can we find out?" (own line) then "Press the buttons below."
- "Everything else we build here from nothing." -> "We will build the intuition here from scratch."

What the pairs have in common: complete spoken sentences even when short;
sequencing words (Next, Now, Then, However); a question handed to the reader
before an instruction; the plain sincere version instead of the clever one;
one idea restated in plainer words rather than compressed; first person plural
for the journey; loose grammar left alone.
