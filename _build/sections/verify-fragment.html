Credential check

Verify a credential

Every r-statistics.co certificate carries a public ID. Paste it below to see the holder, the track, the assessment score and the issue date — straight from the record, no account and no contact required.

IDs look like RST-2026-A7K3F9 — RST, a year, then six letters or digits.

Checking…

Public and permanent

Each credential lives at its own URL that anyone can open. The result reflects the live record, including whether it has been revoked.

Tied to real work

A credential is only issued after the holder solves the exercises and passes a code-based assessment. The score shown is the score they earned.

No account needed

Recruiters and hiring managers can check any ID without signing up. Paste, press Verify, and read the result for yourself.
